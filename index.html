What We Do

Borehole Drilling

Professional drilling with our latest Warrior machine, capable of delivering 350m and above with ease. From initial site preparation through casing, well development, and testing, we build dependable boreholes for homes, businesses, farms, and communities.

Geophysical Survey

Accurate underground water detection and site analysis before drilling. Our detailed investigations help identify promising drilling points, estimate suitable depths, and reduce the risk of unsuccessful wells.

Consultancies

Expert advice on water resource management and project feasibility. We support clients with practical planning, technical guidance, project design, and supervision for sound water-infrastructure decisions.

Solar Pump Installation

Sustainable and cost-effective solar-powered water pumping systems. We size, install, and integrate reliable solar pump solutions that provide consistent water while helping to lower ongoing energy costs.
