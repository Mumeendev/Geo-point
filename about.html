About Geopoint Drilling

Your trusted partner in professional water engineering across Nigeria.

Who We Are

Geopoint Drilling Company Nigeria Ltd is a premier water engineering firm specializing in professional borehole drilling, geophysical surveys, and sustainable water solutions. With years of experience and a deep understanding of the Nigerian terrain, we have built a reputation for excellence, reliability, and technical proficiency.

Our mission is to provide clean, accessible water through innovative technology and expert craftsmanship. We believe that water is a fundamental right, and our goal is to ensure that every project we undertake delivers a lasting and efficient water supply system.

Our Core Values

  • Integrity: We operate with transparency and honesty in all our dealings.
  • Quality: We use only the best materials and equipment for every project.
  • Innovation: We continuously adopt new technologies for better results.
  • Safety: We prioritize the safety of our team and our clients above all else.
